Welcome

Welcome to the Northgate Public Library. Our doors are open every day until midnight during exam season. Please return your books on time so that other members of the community can borrow them tonight or tomorrow.

The library sits near the old stone bridge on River Street. Free parking is available behind the main building after six in the evening. Bring your card to the front desk and a friendly member of staff will help you find anything you need.

This Week

Join our reading group on Thursday, meet new friends, and discuss a new novel each month. Children can attend story time in the morning, while teens gather in the afternoon to study together and share quiet space.

A special lecture on local history will begin at seven near the garden. Seats are limited, so please arrive early and wait by the entrance if the room is full. We hope to see you soon at the library.
